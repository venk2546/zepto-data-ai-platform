Membership Tiers

Zepto offers membership plans that may provide additional benefits to eligible customers.

Membership benefits can vary depending on the selected plan.

Benefits may include special offers, discounts, delivery-related benefits, or other promotional advantages.

The available membership plans and their prices are displayed to customers in the application.

Customers should review the current membership details before subscribing.

Membership benefits are subject to the terms and conditions applicable to the selected plan.

A membership does not guarantee that every product or order will receive a discount.

Customers can check their membership status and available benefits through their account.
