Damaged and Missing Items

Customers should check their order after delivery.

If an item is damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible.

The customer may be required to provide details about the affected item.

Photographs may be requested when necessary to verify a damaged or incorrect item.

After reviewing the issue, the support team may provide an appropriate resolution such as a refund, replacement, or other eligible solution.

The final resolution depends on the circumstances of the order and applicable policy.

Customers should keep the relevant order information available when contacting support.
