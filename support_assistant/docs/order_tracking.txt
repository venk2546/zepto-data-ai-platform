Order Tracking

Customers can track an active order using the order tracking feature.

After an order is successfully placed, the customer can view the current order status.

Order statuses may change as the order moves through the fulfillment and delivery process.

The estimated delivery time may change because of traffic, weather, order volume, item availability, or other operational conditions.

If the order appears to be delayed, customers should first check the latest status in the application.

If additional assistance is required, customers can contact customer support with their order details.

Tracking information is available for eligible active orders.
