Gift Cards

Gift cards can be used according to the terms and conditions associated with the specific gift card.

Customers should check the gift card details for its value, validity period, and applicable restrictions.

A gift card may be redeemable only through supported Zepto channels.

Gift cards should be kept secure because the code may be required for redemption.

If a gift card is not working, customers should contact customer support and provide the relevant gift card and transaction details when requested.

Gift card usage may be subject to eligibility and promotional restrictions.

Gift card terms may change, so customers should check the current information provided with the gift card.
