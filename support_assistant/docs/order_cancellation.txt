Order Cancellation

Customers may be able to cancel an order before it reaches a stage where cancellation is no longer possible.

Cancellation availability depends on the current status of the order.

If the cancellation option is available in the application, customers can use that option to request cancellation.

Once an order has progressed too far in the fulfillment or delivery process, cancellation may not be available.

If a cancellation is successfully processed, any applicable refund will be handled according to the payment and refund policy.

Customers should check the order status before attempting cancellation.

If the customer cannot cancel an eligible order through the application, they can contact customer support for assistance.
