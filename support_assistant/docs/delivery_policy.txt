Delivery Policy

Zepto provides fast delivery for eligible orders.

Standard delivery is normally completed within 10 to 30 minutes, depending on location, traffic, weather, item availability, and order volume.

Customers can view the estimated delivery time before placing an order.

Some locations may have different delivery times based on operational availability.

Customers should ensure that the delivery address and contact information are correct when placing an order.

If a delivery is delayed, the customer can check the order status through the order tracking option or contact customer support.

Delivery charges, if applicable, are displayed during checkout before the order is placed.
