Returns and Refunds

Customers may request a return or refund for eligible items according to the applicable return policy.

Return eligibility depends on the product type and condition of the item.

Items that are damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing may qualify for a refund or replacement after verification.

Customers should report an issue as soon as possible after receiving the order.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other information to verify a damaged or incorrect item.

Refunds are processed after the request is reviewed and approved.

The refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method.

Some products may be non-returnable because of their nature or applicable policy restrictions.

Customers can contact customer support if they need help with a return or refund request.
