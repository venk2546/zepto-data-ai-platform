Customer Support Hours

Zepto customer support is available through the supported customer service channels.

Customers can use the application to access available support options.

Support availability may vary depending on the type of issue and the support channel being used.

For urgent order-related issues, customers should first check the order status and available help options in the application.

When contacting customer support, customers should provide relevant order information so that the support team can investigate the issue.

Support requests may require additional information depending on the nature of the problem.

Customers should use the currently displayed support hours and contact options in the application for the latest availability information.
